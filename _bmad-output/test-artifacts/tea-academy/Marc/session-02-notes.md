## Key Concepts Covered

1. **Testing as Engineering:** Design before write, maintain like production code, risk-based decisions
2. **Risk-Based Testing:** P0 (critical) → P1 (high) → P2 (medium) → P3 (low); Probability × Impact
3. **Definition of Done:** No flaky tests, no hard waits, stateless & parallelizable, self-cleaning, low maintenance, tests near source
4. **Quality standards** = engineering rigor, not perfectionism
